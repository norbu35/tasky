# Product Management Analysis: Tasky PRD (v1.3)

## 1. Critical Errors of Judgment (Showstoppers)

**A. App Store Policy Violation (Monetization Mechanics)**
*   **The Issue:** In Phase 2 and 3, the PRD relies heavily on QPay for all monetization, including: Credit Packs (`REQ-PAY-19`), Promoted Listings (`REQ-PAY-23`), and Subscriptions (`REQ-PAY-30`).
*   **The Error:** Apple and Google strictly prohibit using third-party payment gateways (like QPay) for **digital goods and services consumed within the app**. While physical services (cleaning, escrow) can use QPay, platform features like *lead-unlock credits*, *profile boosts*, and *premium placements* must use native In-App Purchases (IAP), subject to the 15-30% App Store tax. If submitted as written, the app will be rejected during App Store Review.

**B. Identity Strategy Actively Excludes the Target Supply (Phase 0-1)**
*   **The Issue:** `REQ-AUTH-01` mandates Facebook OAuth as the *exclusive* login method for Phase 0-1.
*   **The Error:** Appendix `[F5]` explicitly notes: *"Supply side includes phone-first workers; Facebook-only auth excludes some Taskers"*. By enforcing Facebook-only auth, you are actively blocking your most critical persona (informal laborers, ger-district workers) during the most fragile phase of the marketplace (cold start). Furthermore, Unegui (the incumbent) operates on phone numbers. A "trust-centric" platform cannot launch with Facebook-only auth, as FB accounts are easily faked or duplicated, whereas local phone numbers are scarce and traceable.

**C. Fixed Budget Guesswork on Complex Categories**
*   **The Issue:** `REQ-TASK-01` requires the Customer to set a fixed budget upfront. There is no mechanism for Taskers to negotiate or submit counter-offers.
*   **The Error:** While fixed pricing works for standardized tasks (e.g., 2-room apartment cleaning), forcing a user to guess the budget for "Plumbing" or "Electrical" is a conversion killer. If a user sets the budget too low out of ignorance, the task sits empty for 120 minutes before the "rescue flow" (`REQ-BOOK-09`) prompts them to raise it. Two hours is an eternity in on-demand services; the customer will leave for Unegui within 20 minutes.

## 2. Significant Product Gaps

**A. Tax and Compliance (E-Barimt)**
*   **The Gap:** There is zero mention of Mongolia's mandatory VAT receipt system (E-Barimt). 
*   **The Impact:** This is a massive legal and product gap, especially for the **B2B Lite** segment in Phase 2 (`REQ-PAY-25`). Businesses (Airbnb hosts, offices) *require* E-Barimt to write off expenses. If Tasky cannot issue an E-Barimt for the platform fees or the service itself, B2B adoption will stall entirely.

**B. Asymmetric Risk in the Credit System**
*   **The Gap:** Under `REQ-PAY-13`, spent credits are only refunded if the *Customer* cancels. However, the exact task location is hidden until *after* the Tasker unlocks the lead (`REQ-LEAK-03`). 
*   **The Impact:** If a Tasker pays to unlock a lead, sees the exact address is unserviceable (e.g., completely out of their transport range), or talks to the customer and realizes the scope is completely misrepresented, the Tasker must cancel. Under the current rules, the Tasker loses their money (credits) AND gets a cancellation penalty (`REQ-BOOK-06`). This will cause massive supply-side churn and immediate resentment toward the monetization model.

**C. Consequence-Free Non-Payment in Phase 1**
*   **The Gap:** In Phase 1, `REQ-SAFE-03` states disputes are "evidence-only mediation" with no monetary compensation.
*   **The Impact:** If a Tasker completes a 4-hour cleaning job and the Customer refuses to pay (direct settlement), the only consequence is an "internal misconduct note" on the Customer's profile. The Tasker is left unpaid. The PRD claims to solve the "trust" problem of Facebook groups, but in Phase 1, the platform offers no actual protection for the supply side.

**D. Solo Founder Capacity Fallacy**
*   **The Gap:** `Section 10.3` caps founder manual ops at 10 hours/week. Yet, the founder is expected to manually backstop 30 bookings (`REQ-ADMIN-07`), manually review ID/Selfie verifications for 50+ Taskers within a 24h SLA (`REQ-SAFE-01`), and manage disputes. 
*   **The Impact:** 10 hours a week is roughly 1.4 hours a day. The math on the operational overhead does not balance. Concierge execution of a single moving or cleaning gig could take 3-4 hours alone.

## 3. Discrepancies and Edge Cases

*   **Escrow Incentive Misalignment:** `REQ-PAY-32` introduces opt-in Escrow. `REQ-PAY-34` slaps a massive 10-15% platform fee on it. Why would a Customer and Tasker mutually opt into a 15% tax when they are already connected via the platform and can just settle directly via QPay for free (Phase 2 behavior)? The PRD lacks a compelling "carrot" to drive escrow adoption.
*   **No-Show Verification is Too Rigid:** `REQ-BOOK-11` dictates a No-Show can only be flagged if "neither party has posted an in-app status update/check-in in the preceding 30 minutes". If the Tasker arrives and immediately starts working without tapping "check in" on the app, the Customer could maliciously flag a No-Show to avoid platform history/fees. The physical reality of gig work often outpaces app state updates.

## Recommended Next Steps for v1.4

1.  **Pivot Auth:** Swap Phase 0-1 to Phone/SMS OTP. Delay Facebook OAuth to Phase 2. Use a cheap local SMS gateway (e.g., CallPro) to keep costs low. You cannot build a trust-centric app without verified phone numbers from Day 1.
2.  **Redesign Monetization Rails:** Map out what is an IAP vs. what is a QPay transaction to survive App Store Review. (Hint: Lead credits = Apple/Google IAP; Escrow Service Payment = QPay).
3.  **Introduce Bidding/Estimates:** For non-standard categories (Plumbing/Handyman), change the pricing UX from "Fixed Budget" to "Tasker Estimates" or "Hourly Rate".
4.  **Fix the Credit Refund Loophole:** Implement a "Mutual Cancel / Scope Mismatch" workflow where Taskers can recoup credits if the job was misrepresented.
