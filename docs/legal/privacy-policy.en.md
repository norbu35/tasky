# Privacy Policy (English)

**Last updated:** 2026-05-11 | **Version:** 1.0

Tasky LLC ("we", "us", "our") operates the Tasky mobile application and website (collectively, the "Service"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use the Service.

## 1. Data Controller

Tasky LLC, registered in Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia, is the data controller responsible for your personal data.

**Contact:** privacy@tasky.mn

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information you provide

- **Account information:** Phone number, display name, profile photo, biography
- **Identity verification:** Government-issued ID images, registration number (for tasker verification)
- **Task information:** Task descriptions, location addresses and coordinates, photos
- **Messages:** Content of direct messages between users
- **Payment information:** Transaction records (we do not store full payment card details)

### 2.2 Information collected automatically

- **Device information:** Device model, operating system version, push notification token
- **Usage data:** Feature usage analytics, crash reports
- **Log data:** Server logs with anonymized identifiers for debugging and security

### 2.3 Information from third parties

- **Facebook:** When you sign in with Facebook, we receive your Facebook user ID and basic profile information as permitted by your Facebook privacy settings

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information to:

- Create and manage your account
- Match customers with taskers and facilitate task booking
- Enable messaging between users
- Process identity verification for taskers
- Send push notifications about bookings, messages, and account activity
- Monitor service quality and prevent fraud
- Comply with legal obligations
- Improve our Service through analytics

## 4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your data under the following legal bases:

- **Contract performance:** To deliver the Service you signed up for
- **Consent:** For push notifications and optional data collection
- **Legitimate interest:** For fraud prevention, security, and service improvement
- **Legal obligation:** For identity verification and regulatory compliance

## 5. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

- **Service providers:** Cloud hosting, push notification delivery, and analytics providers
- **Other users:** Task location (approximate) and profile information visible to matched users
- **Legal authorities:** When required by Mongolian law or court order

## 6. Data Retention

| Data type             | Retention period                                |
| --------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Account data          | Until account deletion                          |
| Task and booking data | Until account deletion                          |
| Messages              | Until conversation deletion or account deletion |
| Government ID images  | Until account deletion                          |
| Analytics             | Aggregated within 90 days                       |
| Audit logs            | Indefinite (immutable, used for security)       |

## 7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

- Access your personal data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Delete your account and associated data
- Withdraw consent for optional processing
- Data portability (receive your data in a structured format)

To exercise these rights, contact privacy@tasky.mn or use the in-app account deletion feature.

## 8. Cross-Border Transfers

Your data is stored on servers that may be located outside Mongolia. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for any cross-border transfer, including standard contractual clauses where applicable.

## 9. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If we learn that we have collected data from a person under 18, we will delete it promptly.

## 10. Data Security

We implement technical and organizational measures to protect your data:

- Phone numbers are encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM)
- All data in transit is encrypted via TLS
- Access controls limit data exposure to authorized personnel
- Regular security audits and penetration testing

## 11. Changes to This Policy

We will notify you of material changes to this Privacy Policy via in-app notification or email. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance.

## 12. Contact

For privacy inquiries: privacy@tasky.mn
