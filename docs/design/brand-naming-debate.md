## Debate 1 Results

### Round 1 — Opening Positions

**Claude Sonnet — PRO Tasky.mn:**
> Clarity beats cleverness. Tasky communicates function instantly. The .mn TLD localizes an otherwise globally styled name. International scalability is not threatened. The growing bilingual urban demographic in Ulaanbaatar are the primary early adopters. Burden of proof lies with anyone proposing to change it.

**Codex (GPT-5.4) — LEAN AGAINST:**
> Tasky is competent but not optimal for trust-first positioning. It feels lightweight and transactional — indistinguishable from a todo app or freelancer board. It risks sounding imported rather than built for Mongolian households. It can work as a working name, but the company will need to compensate through design and messaging.

**Claude Opus — AGAINST:**
> Tasky is pure English tech-speak that signals to-do list app, not trusted human services. The .mn TLD is an asset being squandered on a generic English stem. Top alternative: Gar.mn (hand/helping hand — short, powerful, culturally rooted, internationally clean).

### Round 2 — Rebuttals

**Sonnet:** Opponents conflate brand name with brand identity. Trust is built through product, not phonology. Airbnb/Uber/Stripe succeeded without descriptive names. The Mongolian alternatives carry their own problems — "Gar" means exit/body part/directional particle depending on context. Mongolian consumers already use English-origin digital product names.

**Codex:** Sonnet overstates functional clarity. "Clarity beats cleverness" only works if the name is clear to the target market — Tasky is startup-flavored English, not Mongolian. But Opus is too confident about poetic alternatives without proving commercial strength. Decisive position: keep Tasky.mn only if validated by user testing against stronger candidates.

**Opus:** Sonnet misapplies the Airbnb/Stripe argument — those names succeeded with billions in marketing. A Mongolian startup must let the name carry trust before the product does. Change now is cheap. Change after launch is a rebranding project.

### Debate 1 Verdict
Codex's nuanced position is most defensible: Tasky.mn is functional but not optimal. Proceed with it as working name; test alternatives before permanent brand commitment.

- **Gojek:** Vernacular Indonesian transport term + "Go-" prefix. Hyper-local, instantly understood.
- **Meituan:** 美团 = "beautiful together/good things shared." Cultural resonance in 2 characters.

- Pattern: winning names either root deeply in local culture OR use global-neutral English. Hybrid names often fail at both.

### Why Tasky.mn Should Remain Working Name Only

1. Generic English tech-speak — could be a to-do app, a freelancer board, or a productivity tool
2. Does no cultural work in a trust-first market where strangers enter homes
3. The .mn TLD is the only Mongolian signal — the stem itself is imported
4. No competitive moat: indistinguishable from TaskRabbit derivatives
5. Change now (pre-launch) is cheap. Post-launch rebranding costs: SEO, recall, legal, team alignment

### Recommended Next Steps

1. **Keep Tasky.mn as working name** — it's functional and embedded in the codebase
2. **Register Itgel.mn** before a competitor does (< 10,000 .mn domains registered; it's likely available)
3. **Commission 10–15 user interviews in Ulaanbaatar** — test Itgel.mn vs. Tasky.mn vs. Sanaa.mn
4. **Make final brand decision before first public launch or investor deck**
