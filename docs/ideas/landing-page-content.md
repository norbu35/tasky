# Landing Page Content Ideas

**Date:** 2026-03-21
**Goal:** Brainstorm high-impact content sections to attract and convert both customers and taskers, adhering to the project's core principles of building local trust and marketplace liquidity.

---

### 1. Popular Service Categories (The "Aha!" Moment)
Users often don't know what they can delegate until they see it. 
*   **What it is:** A visual, interactive grid or carousel of the most popular services (e.g., Deep Cleaning, Handyman Repairs, Errands & Delivery, Tech Support).
*   **Why it works:** It immediately answers "What can I use this for?" and provides quick deep-links to specific flows.

### 2. The Tasky Trust & Safety Guarantee
Since local trust is the biggest hurdle for a domestic marketplace, we need to address it head-on.
*   **What it is:** A dedicated, premium-looking section highlighting our vetting process. 
*   **Content:** Bullet points emphasizing ID Verification (KYC), secure Escrow Payments (money is held until the job is done), and our verified review system. We could include a custom "Verified Tasky Safety" shield icon.

### 3. Local Social Proof & Testimonials
Generic tech testimonials don't work; we need relatable, local stories.
*   **What it is:** 3–4 stylized review cards featuring "real" scenarios (e.g., a busy working parent in Ulaanbaatar who found a cleaner, or a local business that hired a last-minute delivery driver).
*   **Why it works:** People trust platforms that their peers use. It builds emotional connection and undeniable social proof.

### 4. "Mobile-First" App Download Banner
The PRD states this is a mobile-first ecosystem.
*   **What it is:** A high-contrast, visually distinct section near the footer showing a mockup of the Tasky mobile app in-hand.
*   **Content:** App Store / Google Play badges and a QR code that users can scan directly from their desktop screen to download the app instantly.

### 5. Tasker Earning Spotlight (Supply-Side Acquisition)
Marketplaces die without liquidity (enough workers). 
*   **What it is:** A section specifically speaking to potential Taskers. E.g., *"Turn your free time into income."*
*   **Content:** Highlighting the benefits like flexible hours, guaranteed payments, and perhaps a spotlight on a "Top Earner" to show that real money can be made safely on the platform.

### 6. Live Marketplace Stats (The "Liquidity" Marker)
*   **What it is:** A sleek, animated numbers counter separating sections.
*   **Content:** E.g., "10,000+ Verified Users", "5,000+ Tasks Completed", "Average 10-Minute Match Time in Ulaanbaatar."
