# Tasky Business Model Strategy & Go-To-Market Plan

## Overview
This document outlines the strategic 4-Phase Go-To-Market and Business Model plan for launching Tasky in the Mongolian market. The core philosophy is to prioritize **liquidity and trust over immediate revenue** to solve the marketplace "Cold Start" problem.

---

### Phase 1: The Liquidity Phase (0% Commission)
**The Goal:** Reach "Liquidity"—the state where a posted task is accepted almost immediately, and a Tasker opening the app consistently finds work.
**The Revenue:** $0 

**Key Execution Steps:**
1. **Constrain the Market (Hyper-Local):** Launch in one specific district or for one specific high-frequency task category (e.g., exclusively "Apartment Cleaning" or "Delivery").
2. **Seed the Supply (Taskers) First:** Manually recruit a core group of 20-30 highly motivated, vetted Taskers. We may need to guarantee them an hourly rate to stay "on call" during launch week.
3. **Subsidize the Demand (Customers):** Aggressively market to customers in the launch district offering "First Task Free" or heavy discounts. *Tasky pays the Tasker their full rate out of startup capital.*
4. **Do Things That Don't Scale:** Call every customer after their task. Help every Tasker set up their profile. If a Tasker cancels, the founding team must show up to do the task to protect the brand.

---

### Phase 2: Soft Monetization & Value-Add
**The Goal:** Prove that users are willing to pay for enhancements without taxing the core transaction.
**The Monetization:** Core matching remains 0% commission, but premium, optional features are introduced.

**Key Execution Steps:**
- **For Taskers (Pro Badges):** Introduce a "Pro Badge" that significantly boosts profile visibility. Taskers pay a nominal one-time processing fee for an advanced background check to earn this badge.
- **For Customers (Priority Matching):** Introduce a small optional fee to blast their task to top-tier, 5-star Taskers before anyone else sees it.

---

### Phase 3: The "Take Rate" (Core Monetization)
**The Goal:** Achieve sustainable unit economics once the platform is buzzing and users rely on it for income and convenience.
**The Monetization:** Introduce a platform commission (Take Rate).

**Key Execution Steps:**
- **Grandfather Early Adopters:** Reward Phase 1 & 2 Taskers by locking them into a low, eternal commission rate (e.g., 5%). Apply standard market rates (e.g., 10-15%) to new Taskers. 
- **Fee Distribution:** The supply side (Taskers) pays the bulk of the commission for lead generation. Customers pay a smaller "Trust & Safety / Processing Fee" (e.g., 3-5%).
- **Combatting Platform Leakage:** Prevent off-app transactions by making the on-app experience invaluable:
  - Secure Escrow payments
  - Platform-provided insurance/protection
  - A robust public review score that dictates a Tasker's future earnings.

---

### Phase 4: Predictable Recurring Revenue (Ecosystem Expansion)
**The Goal:** Move away from purely transactional revenue and lock in high-value, recurring users.
**The Monetization:** Premium Subscriptions and B2B logic.

**Key Execution Steps:**
- **Tasky Plus (For Customers):** A monthly subscription (e.g., 15,000 MNT/month) that waives all Trust & Safety fees and guarantees minimum-time matching (e.g., under 1 hour).
- **Tasky for Business:** Allow local offices, restaurants, or retail shops to use Tasky to hire temporary dishwashers, office cleaners, or movers, invoicing them via standard B2B terms.

---

### Management Philosophy for the Early Stages
1. **Optimize for Speed over Revenue:** In the first 6 months, the "North Star" metrics are **Time-to-Match** and **Repeat Usage**. Revenue is a lagging indicator.
2. **Be the Concierge:** For the first 100 tasks, act like a high-end concierge. Personally match tasks, monitor communication, and intervene to guarantee a 5-star result.
3. **Build the "Trust" Moat:** Every core feature—identity verification, escrow payments, secure messaging—is laying the foundation for trust. Users will only pay the Phase 3 commission if they trust Tasky significantly more than informal classified ads.
