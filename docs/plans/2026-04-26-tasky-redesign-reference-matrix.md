# Tasky Redesign Refero Reference Matrix

**Date:** 2026-04-26
**Status:** Active research matrix for proof-screen design
**Parent thesis:** `docs/plans/2026-04-26-tasky-redesign-thesis.md`

## Research rules

- Use Refero as an evidence source, not as a source of product truth.
- For the mobile proof subset, absorb Airbnb interaction anatomy, spacing rhythm, layout hierarchy, density, sheet behavior,
  card composition, selection states, and QA comparison where those patterns fit Tasky screen specs.
- Record both adopted and rejected patterns.
- Compare web and iOS separately before extracting a shared Tasky pattern.
- Retrieve and inspect actual screenshot content before runtime UI work; search-result metadata and thumbnail URLs are only
  shortlist inputs.
- Do not adopt references that depend on escrow, payment protection, wallet safety, provider tiers, instant match, or
  paid lead unlock.
- Keep Tasky's Phase 1 model explicit: verified taskers may apply, customers choose from open applications, settlement
  is direct, trust is process-backed.

## Airbnb-specific mobile adoption matrix

Airbnb references are UX absorption inputs for interaction anatomy, spacing, hierarchy, density, sheet behavior, card
composition, selection states, and QA comparison. Tasky keeps native primitives, Tasky tokens, i18n-backed copy,
PRD-owned trust rules, open applications, direct settlement, and exact-address visibility only after confirmation.

| Tasky mobile surface | Airbnb structure to adopt                                                                                                                                         | Tasky implementation boundary                                                                                                              | Explicit rejection                                                                                          |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Customer posting     | Guided creation rhythm, progressive disclosure, bottom-sheet pickers, review-before-post summary, sticky CTA, and edit-in-place summary rows.                     | Applies across `SCR-CUST-002` through `SCR-CUST-008`; posting remains structured form data and does not introduce runtime AI posting.      | No instant booking, checkout, deposits, payment protection, or travel-style guarantee copy.                 |
| Tasker feed          | Filter sheet, active-filter summary, result-count CTA, no-results remediation, and denser cards with location, timing, quote/budget, applications, and freshness. | Applies to `SCR-TASK-001`; filters change the local view and do not rank or gate taskers.                                                  | No map-first browsing, promoted listings, "best task" ranking, or applicant-count competition framing.      |
| Applicant review     | Structured comparison cards with eligibility, verification, response quality, quote, timing, completed jobs, and review-threshold evidence.                       | Applies to `SCR-CUST-009`, `SCR-CUST-011`, and `SCR-CUST-013`; customers choose from all applicants and public rating remains thresholded. | No "recommended" or "best match" cue, provider tiers, swipe selection, or open pre-booking chat.            |
| Booking detail       | Timeline-first layout, address visibility note, direct-settlement payment note, completion/cancellation actions, and support reason sheets.                       | Applies to `SCR-CUST-017` and `SCR-TASK-013`; recourse routes through Tasky booking state and existing dispute/support flows.              | No live trip tracking, refund guarantees, insurance/protection framing, or hidden payment rails.            |
| Profile and reviews  | Review summary/search/filter scaffolding, anchored review sections, and neutral low-review state before aggregate rating is public.                               | Applies to `SCR-SHARED-012` and `SCR-CUST-013`; aggregate ratings appear only when the PRD threshold is met.                               | No superhost-style badges, percentile labels, reputation ranking, or unsupported rating methodology claims. |

## Pattern decisions

### Adopt

| Decision                                                                  | Reason                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Applies to                                         |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Use sticky booking summaries on web proof screens                         | Fresha, Acuity, and appointment-cancellation references preserve service, schedule, policy, and next actions while users decide or manage bookings.                                                    | Customer posting, applicant review, booking detail |
| Use bottom sticky CTAs on mobile proof screens                            | Fresha and Airbnb mobile booking references keep the next action reachable while service details, date choices, and trust information scroll.                                                          | Posting, task detail, booking detail               |
| Use compact metadata cards for tasker work discovery                      | LinkedIn and X job references show that title, location, time, status, and apply affordance can be scanned quickly when kept in a tight hierarchy.                                                     | Tasker feed, filter-backed open tasks              |
| Use focused application modals or sheets                                  | Parallel and Contra flows keep opportunity context beside a short application response and finish with an explicit applied state.                                                                      | Task detail, application submission                |
| Use PRD-owned review-threshold states before prominent reputation modules | Refero examples show rich review modules where review content exists; Tasky's minimum review threshold comes from PRD policy, so launch needs neutral low-review handling until that threshold is met. | Public profile, applicant review                   |
| Use destructive confirmation with reason capture and durable after-states | IKEA, Fresha, Acuity, and West Elm cancellation flows make irreversible actions deliberate and then reflect canceled or pending status clearly.                                                        | Booking cancellation, no-show, dispute entry       |
| Route recourse through contextual support modules                         | Omio and Airbnb support references show booking lookup, support categories, chat/media attachment, and empty-result alternatives at the point of need.                                                 | Booking detail, dispute support, help center       |

### Reject

| Decision                                                                         | Reason                                                                                                                                                            | Applies to                                      |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Reject provider ranking or tier badges unless future governing docs approve them | Tasky Phase 1 allows verification-gated eligibility and PRD-thresholded reputation display, not inferred provider tiers, ranking ladders, or top-provider labels. | Applicant review, public profile                |
| Reject escrow, wallet, deposit, and payment-protection language                  | Fresha deposits, Omio protection add-ons, and subscription/account payment surfaces depend on product rails outside Phase 1.                                      | Posting, booking detail, trust copy             |
| Reject instant-book and automatic matching patterns                              | Airbnb instant booking and appointment reserve flows conflict with Tasky's open application model where customers choose among verified taskers.                  | Posting, applicant review, booking confirmation |
| Reject corporate recruiting and social-network tone                              | ATS, LinkedIn recruiter, resume, EEO, premium, applicant-count, and profile-boost patterns create the wrong marketplace posture for Tasky.                        | Tasker feed, application flow                   |
| Reject legal-text-only recourse                                                  | Terms pages explain policy but do not give users a task-specific path to resolve cancellations, complaints, evidence, or support needs.                           | Dispute support, booking cancellation           |
| Reject broad guarantee copy                                                      | SeatGeek-style assurance language and travel-protection framing would imply protections Tasky does not provide in Phase 1.                                        | Trust modules, cancellation policy              |
